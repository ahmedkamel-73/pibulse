About PIBULSE

PIBULSE is a free, skill-based price prediction game built for the Pi Network ecosystem.

What we do: Users predict whether PI price will go UP or DOWN in the next 60 seconds. Predictions are based on live CoinGecko data and stored transparently on our Cloudflare server.

Not Gambling: No Pi payment is required to play. Every user gets 10 free points daily. This is a skill and timing game.

Transparency: All predictions, round start price, and winners are public at /api/leaderboard and /api/round

Wallet: We use Pi SDK Authentication to link your Pi username and wallet_address to identify real winners. We never ask for your passphrase.

Rewards: Top players on the leaderboard receive Pi from our App Wallet (GD2C...RYDO) via official Pi Payments API.
