Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

1. Nature of Service

PIBULSE is a free-to-play, skill-based prediction game. It is not gambling, betting, or a financial service.

2. No Payments Required

You can play with free daily points. We do not require Pi payments to participate.

3. Pi Authentication

We use Pi SDK (username, wallet_address, payments) to identify you. We store only your Pi username, uid, and public wallet address to determine winners. We never collect your private key or passphrase.

4. Privacy

We store: Pi username, public wallet, predictions, and timestamps. This data is public for transparency in /api/round. We do not sell your data. You can request deletion by contacting us.

5. Fair Play

Winner is determined automatically by server comparing round start price vs current live PI price from CoinGecko. No manipulation.

6. Pi Ad Network

We display Pi Ads to support the app. Ads are shown via official Pi.Ads SDK.

7. Rewards

Rewards are sent from our App Wallet using Pi Payments API. Pi Network controls final transaction.

8. Contact

For questions: via Pi Network app listing contact.
