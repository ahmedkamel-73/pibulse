Privacy Policy - PIBULSE

Last updated: May 2026

1. What we collect

When you connect your Pi Wallet, we receive your Pi username, wallet address (public key) and UID from Pi SDK via Pi.authenticate(['username','payments','wallet_address']). We do NOT collect your private key or passphrase.

2. How we use it

We use username and wallet to save your predictions, points (10/10), and leaderboard. Payments (Test-Pi for verification) are processed via Pi Blockchain, we only store paymentId and txid for verification.

3. Storage

Data is stored in Cloudflare KV / D1 and localStorage in your browser (pibulse_user, pibulse_v2_pts). No data is sold.

4. Pi Ad Network

We may show Pi Ads via Pi SDK. Pi may collect ad interaction per their policy.
